# Code of Conduct

Regtend welcomes people of every background and experience level. This applies
to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Working together

- Be respectful and patient, especially when someone is learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and communication styles.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy; never share someone else's personal information without consent.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks,
and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For public project content that violates this policy, you may notify the
maintainer, [@JohanLindvall](https://github.com/JohanLindvall), in the affected
thread if you are comfortable doing so. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
To report abusive content privately to GitHub, use the content's **Report content**
menu or [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Reports sent to GitHub are handled by GitHub, not the project maintainer.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy, ask
participants to change their behaviour, or restrict participation for repeated
or serious violations. Moderation should consider context and be proportionate
to the behaviour.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
